About

When I was 12 years old, I built my own desktop computer so I could run Fortnite faster.

Since this first introduction to learning about technology, I've spent the following years exploring web design and eventually fell in love with the more creative side---especially how precise and accessible design can completely transform someone's experience, even beyond digital interfaces.

Today, I'm a student at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, studying Information Science with a focus in HCI Design, and minoring in Media & Journalism for interactive media. I plan on graduating in May 2027.

Outside of design, you'll find me playing guitar and piano, listening to 2010s music, cooking tasty meals, and watching (and analyzing) (and once in a while, filming) movies.
